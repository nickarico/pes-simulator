Teaching note

Copper and chromium provide evidence of ground-state configurations that differ from a simple Aufbau filling prediction. Compare each to its neighbors to see why experimental evidence matters.

Scientific model: This is a deliberately simplified threshold-discovery simulation, not a laboratory PES instrument. Binding energies are approximate educational model values, not a calibrated experimental dataset. Each energy setting represents fresh neutral atoms; the displayed electron counts represent relative subshell peak intensities, not electrons stripped sequentially from one atom. Real PES peak positions, spin-orbit splitting, cross sections, and detection efficiency are more complex. Subshell populations and the Cr/Cu configurations follow accepted ground-state configurations. The graph uses a logarithmic binding-energy axis.
